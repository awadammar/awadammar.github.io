I'm a backend engineer with over five years building and scaling distributed systems, currently focused on LLM gateways and agentic AI platforms. At Intelmatix, I architected the backend for MATIX, a new agentic AI platform, the auth, identity, and real-time streaming boundary every LLM interaction routes through, using Kotlin, Spring Boot, Go, LiteLLM, and Keycloak. I also work on an agentic system for the Royal Commission that manages over 40,000 vehicles and helps serve more than 1.8 million pilgrims, and I own the backend of EDIX, a multi-tenant AI SaaS platform running across five microservices on AWS.

Before this, I spent three years at BMW Group in Cairo. I re-architected a logging service to process asynchronously, cutting response time and eliminating a real chunk of performance-related losses. I also built a VS Code extension for an internal DSL that hit 2,500+ downloads in its first month, and tuned JVM parameters and garbage collection to speed up a tool used by 50,000 engineers by over 45%.

Earlier, at Lens EDA, I worked on backend systems for chip design software: optimizing SQL queries and WebSocket handling for large datasets, cutting database load by 90%, and restructuring our CI suite to run end-to-end tests 75% faster.

I studied Computer Engineering at Ain Shams University, graduating in the top 10% of my class. My graduation project used a Transformer-based neural machine translation model to convert Android app source code to iOS and back, which I later published at IEEE NILES. I've also contributed to Apache Log4j, and I like mentoring engineers newer to the field.
